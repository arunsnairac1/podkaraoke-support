PodKaraoke

Privacy policy

PodKaraoke · Updated 19 November 2026

In short: PodKaraoke never collects, shares or transmits your songs, lyrics, recordings or personal information. Everything happens on your device, and everything it keeps stays on your device.

Your microphone and recordings

PodKaraoke asks for the microphone only to record your singing while a song plays. Recordings are processed on your device (voice clean-up, levelling, mixing) and kept in the app's own storage as your saved takes. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Deleting a take, or the app, removes it.

Your songs and lyrics

Songs you pick are copied into the app's own storage so they can be played and processed. The song's title, artist and cover are read from the file itself; nothing is looked up online. Lyrics you import or paste are stored on your device with the song. Separating the vocals from the music and telling singers apart are done by machine-learning models that run entirely on your device.

Sharing and saving

Nothing leaves PodKaraoke unless you share it. When you press a mix to M4A or make a lyrics video, the file is made on your device and handed to the share sheet, to the app you choose. On Android a copy is also saved to your phone's Music or Movies folder (in a PodKaraoke folder), where you can delete it like any other file.

No servers, no data collection

PodKaraoke has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We (the developer) receive no personal information, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers. The Android app has no internet permission at all, and the iPhone app sends nothing anywhere. If you choose to share crash reports with Apple or Google through your device's settings, that is handled by them under their privacy policies.

Children

PodKaraoke collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
