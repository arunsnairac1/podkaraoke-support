PodKaraoke

Sing over any song you own.

PodKaraoke lifts the vocals off a song right on your phone, shows the lyrics in time, records you while the music plays in your headphones, and mixes your take so you can share it as a song or a lyrics video.

How it works

Pick a song from your phone. On-device machine learning separates the singer from the music (once per song). If the song is a duet, PodKaraoke tells the voices apart so you can sing just your part. Add the song's synced lyrics (an .lrc file, or paste them from a site like lrclib.net), put your headphones on, and tap Sing.

Afterwards, the mix screen cleans up your voice (breaths, mouth clicks, background hiss), sets it to sit with the music, and lets you adjust levels, reverb and timing before you press it to an M4A or a lyrics video.

Testing the Android app

PodKaraoke for Android is in testing on Google Play. To join: become a member of the PodKaraoke Testers group with the Google account you use on your phone, then open the testing link you were sent and tap Become a tester.
